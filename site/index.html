02 · About

Both sides of the data

I'm an analytics engineer and backend software engineer. I work on the services that produce data and on the models that make it useful.

On the backend I build APIs with Django, FastAPI and Go: payment orchestration over M-Pesa, async transcription with background workers, multi-tenant WhatsApp messaging and feature-flag services. I design for idempotent writes, retries with backoff, hashed API keys and signed webhooks.

On the analytics side I take raw data through dbt staging, intermediate and mart layers on DuckDB, add data tests, and publish dashboards that answer a specific business question.
